It was in the year 2642 that king Telnor had word from his kinsman Angrist in
the land of Vanyar beyond the seas. One of Angrist's vassals, Draupner, had
risen in revolt and with the help of a dragon had invaded the northern part of
Angrist's realm and stolen the Evenstar, the priceless diamond the dwarves found
deep under the mountains at the beginning of time. Angrist asked for aid, and
Telnor sent you, since you are held to be fearless and deadly. With you came
also Teleri, a wise elf with great knowledge of powers that work but cannot be
seen with the naked eye. After a long voyage the ship cast anchor off Angrist's
castle.
